How it works

Add your classes, share when you’re free, and match with classmates who get your courses.

  1. Add your schedule Enter courses by hand or snap a schedule photo — FASU can read it so you don’t retype every line.
  2. Find a buddy Match by course, major, study style, and shared availability — including classmates who share your professors across sections.
  3. Study together Join Study Now, host sessions, use flashcards, or find peer tutoring — all with verified students at your school.

Built for campus study life

  • Course matching Find classmates in the same courses and major — not random strangers from a feed.
  • Study Now See who’s studying live, join a session, or post when you’re open.
  • Flashcards & tools Build private decks for your own courses and keep study tools next to your buddies.
  • Accountability Streaks, hours, and session history so your week stays honest.
  • Tutoring discovery Offer free help, or list paid rates with FASU+. Payments between students stay off-app.
  • Campus spots & rooms Meet in person on campus or jump into a virtual room when you can’t be there.

Quick answers

What is FASU?

FASU is Find A Study-Buddy University — a campus app that helps verified college students find classmates to study with by course, schedule, and study style.

Is FASU free?

Yes. Core matching and study tools are free for verified students. Optional FASU+ adds advanced matching and group features for $4.99/mo or $49.99/yr.

Does FASU use AI tutors?

No. FASU connects you with real classmates on your campus.
