Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

FABU (“Find A Book University”) is a campus textbook trading app and a product of Shot Call'a Games, LLC (“we,” “us”), offered as part of the Improvestu initiative. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data — name, email (typically a school/.edu address), university, and profile details you provide.
  • App content — class schedules, textbook listings, trade requests, chat messages, wanted posts, club memberships, and related metadata.
  • Device & usage — basic technical logs needed to run and secure the service (for example app version, crash diagnostics, and authentication events).
  • Ads — if ads are shown, advertising partners (such as Google AdMob) may collect device identifiers and usage data under their own policies.

2. How we use information

  • Provide campus matching, listings, trades, messaging, and related features.
  • Verify eligibility (such as school email) and keep the campus community safer.
  • Send in-app notifications about trades, matches, and wanted-book alerts (including FABU+ alerts where applicable).
  • Improve reliability, prevent abuse, and comply with law.

3. Sharing

Campus listings, profile first names, and trade-related details may be visible to other verified students at your university as part of the product. We use service providers (for example Supabase for authentication/database and Google for ads/analytics where enabled) to operate FABU. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data retention

We keep account and content data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us (see below).

5. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards (encryption in transit, access controls). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Children’s privacy

FABU is intended for college/university students and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your choices

  • Update profile and listings in the app.
  • Delete or deactivate content you control (listings, wanted posts, chats as supported).
  • Request account deletion by emailing us.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: improvestu@gmail.com
