Student-facing features

List what you’re finishing. Find what you need next. Keep trades between classmates — FABU never takes a cut of student-to-student money.

  • Campus book board Browse swaps and sales from your university — not a nationwide marketplace of strangers.
  • Schedule matching Taking now ↔ Planned next helps you find trade partners when courses and professors line up.
  • Propose & chat Work out a swap in Messages, accept when it fits, then meet on campus.
  • Wanted posts & alerts Hunt a title or ISBN and get notified when a matching book hits campus (FABU+).
  • Clubs & comps Campus clubs for study circles, plus advisory sale comps when you list for sale.

Safer campus trades

FABU is a listing and matching layer. Condition, authenticity, and meetup outcomes stay between students — with tools that keep things grounded on campus.

  • School-email community Built for verified university addresses so listings stay tied to real campuses.
  • Meet in person Arrange handoffs in public campus spots you already use between classes.
  • No wallet in the middle FABU doesn’t process peer payments — cash, Venmo, or free swaps stay between you.
  • Report & block Cut off bad actors and flag listings or behavior that doesn’t belong.
  • You control the deal Inspect the book, agree on terms, and walk away if something feels off.
