FARU Groups

The club night that used to live in a GroupMe

FARU Groups gives a club a live hub for the night: who has seats, who needs a ride, when people arrive, when they leave, and a QR at the door so members can post without hunting a chat thread. Groups Pro adds Command — the officer board that shows who is still out, who is Driver on Duty, and what still needs a seat before the leave window.

The club pays. Members still ride free. FARU does not dispatch a driver to the door and does not take a cut of seats.

Use cases

Every night a club already runs

These are the real loops Groups is built for — not a city rideshare, not a dispatched bus.

Athletics

Home game send-off

Outing posts a hub for JQH Arena: arrive 6:30–7:15, leave 10:00–11:30. Members tap I have seats or I need a ride. Listings stay on that club. Browse → Events can filter to Outing so the game night does not drown in hometown trips.

  • Arrive and leave windows on the hub, not a wall of timestamps in chat
  • Open seats and ride needs in one place
  • Still-out later counts only people who checked in as needing a ride

Formals

Homecoming — two clubs, one night

A joint hub lets Outing and Dance open the same send-off. Both clubs see the night. Each seat or need still belongs to the club that posted it. Officers still run their own roster. It is not a merged membership.

  • Joint night without mixing club lists
  • Useful for formals, Greek pairings, and council events
  • Groups can host the hub; Pro adds Command on each side

Weekly rhythm

Club ride series

Friday trailheads, Tuesday service, Sunday rehearsal — a series is a club-owned weekly post, not a member hoping someone remembers to repost. Members offer seats or need a ride against that series. It shows on Clubs and on Browse.

  • The club owns the cadence
  • This week’s seats and needs stay attached
  • Works for orgs that move every week, not only big events

At the door

QR so nobody hunts the link

Tape the hub QR on the table, the bus sign, or the door. Members land on this hub and post seats or a need in two taps. No “what’s the GroupMe again” at 10:40.

  • Same hub the officers already opened
  • Listings stay club-only
  • Tonight card shows the QR to members who already opened the hub

At the door, then as you leave

I’m here, then check-out plans

Hub check-in is the night roster, not Safety “I’m OK.” I’m here is attendance. It opens 2 hours before arrive, not days ahead. Officers see you on the roster. Later, Check-out plans asks how you are leaving. Only Need a ride home stays on still-out. Walking, driving alone, or offering seats drops them off that list. Offering seats opens I have seats on this hub — it does not auto-assign Driver on Duty.

  • I’m here — attendance from 2 hours before arrive, not still-out
  • Need a ride — you stay on still-out
  • Walking home — roster only
  • Driving alone — roster only
  • Driving and can take others — post seats on this hub

Member view

Tonight card — just your night

Members see arrive/leave, the QR, and their seat. They do not see officer gap numbers, leftover-seat tools, or Command. I’m here, then Check-out plans when they leave. That’s the point.

How a night runs

  1. Host opens a hub or series Arrive/leave windows, venue, optional joint club. Groups unlocks this. Pro can save it as a template.
  2. Members post seats or needs From the hub, the QR, or Browse → Events filtered to the club. Listings stay club-scoped.
  3. People tap I’m here, then check-out plans Opens 2 hours before arrive. Attendance first. Need a ride, walk, drive alone, or offer seats when they leave. Only ride-need stays on still-out.
  4. Officers close the gaps (Pro) Command shows still-out and Driver on Duty. Fill leftover seats, ping the leave window, rain-plan the pickup, close the night.

Sober-ride network is not Profile Designated Drivers

Two different layers. Mixing the names is how clubs get confused — so Groups does not use “DD.”

  • Driver on Duty is a Groups Pro roster slot for that night or duty week. Officers set how many they need. Members sign a duty night. Offering seats in check-out plans does not auto-assign this role.
  • Sober-ride network is optional: the club can attach a network a student already started or joined from Profile. That Profile layer is still Designated Drivers — ID, schedule, and the safety request flow. Groups only links it.
  • Safety SOS / I’m OK stays on the free Safety hub. Club check-in does not replace it.

Groups FAQ

What is FARU Groups?

A $9.99/month club plan for event ride hubs and club ride series. Billed to the club on the Play / App Store. Members still post seats, request rides, and join a sober-ride network for free.

What does Groups Pro add?

Officer roles and Command: I’m here attendance, check-out plans, still-out, Driver on Duty, reminders, waitlist, rain plan, advisor digest, campus packet, council, and officer transition. Full walkthrough on the Groups Pro page.

Are hub rides only for that club?

Yes. A Home game send-off on Outing is Outing-only. Joint hubs let a second club open the night; each listing still belongs to the club that posted it.

Is this a dispatched ride for the event?

No. FARU is a campus message board. Students post seats and needs. We do not send a driver to you and we do not take a cut of the seat.

Does I’m here make someone Driver on Duty?

No. I’m here is attendance. “Driving and can take others” on check-out plans opens I have seats on that hub. Officers assign Driver on Duty as a duty slot. Profile Designated Drivers stay on the Safety / Profile flow.

Does FARU email Student Affairs?

No automatic campus email. The packet is an officer export. Send to Student Affairs is a tap only when a campus license stores that inbox.
