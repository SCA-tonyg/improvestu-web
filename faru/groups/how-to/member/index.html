Your night on the hub

FARU Groups is a club/organization night board. Officers close gaps. Students still claim the ride.

You do not pay for Groups. The club does. You still post seats, request rides, tap I’m here, and join a sober-ride network for free. You never see officer gap numbers. FARU+ is a separate student plan — you can hold both.

Join the club

  1. Open Clubs and join with the invite code FARUSEED, FARUDANCE, or your chapter’s code. Banned members cannot rejoin until unbanned.
  2. Open Club → Groups If the club has Groups or Pro, you get hubs. If it has neither, you see a Groups vs Pro comparison — only host or president starts a plan.

Tonight card

Groups Pro members see a Tonight card: hub title, venue, arrive/leave, QR, your seat if you have one, Open hub. You do not see leftover-seat tools, duty gap, or Command.

Scan the QR at the door if you did not already open the hub. Officers see you on the roster after I’m here.

I have seats and I need a ride

Two primary actions. Both stay on this club’s hub — not the campus hometown board.

I have seats

Post leftover seats on this hub. Other members request them like any FARU listing: chat, request, you accept. Check-out plan “Driving and can take others” opens this same action.

I need a ride

Post a ride need on this hub. Drivers on the night can offer. This is a listing, not the Waitlist button.

I’m here — attendance

  1. Wait until 2 hours before arrive Before that, the button is hidden. The card says when I’m here opens. You cannot tap it days in advance.
  2. Tap I’m here You land on the hub roster. Officers see you showed up. This is not still-out.

I’m here is not Safety I’m OK. SOS / I’m OK stay on the free Safety hub for a confirmed trip. Club attendance does not replace that.

Waitlist is not I need a ride

Waitlist means you still need a seat on this club night — a queue for the whole hub, not one car. I need a ride is a public need post on the hub.

If an officer taps Seat or Fill leftover seats, you get “Seat available. Open the hub to claim a ride.” You still pick a listing. Officers do not drop you into a specific car.

Driver on Duty is not your leftover seats

Driver on Duty is a Groups Pro roster slot for the night. You sign a duty night or an officer assigns the slot. Profile Designated Drivers (a code on Safety / Profile) is a different layer. The club can attach that network; it does not become Duty.

What this looks like on a real night

Home game

Post I have seats to the lot, or I need a ride. Scan QR. I’m here after 2 hours before arrive. Need a ride home if you still need a seat out.

Practice

Series already exists. I’m here after rehearsal. Check-out plan if you need a ride or can take others.

Formal

Join waitlist if cars are short. When an officer seats you, open the hub and request a listing — do not wait for a dispatched car.

Joint night

Dance can open Outing’s send-off. Your post still belongs to your club.
