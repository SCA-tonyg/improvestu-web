How-To · Advisor · Groups Pro

See the night. Do not run the cars.

FARU Groups is a club/organization night board. Officers close gaps. Students still claim the ride.

Advisor is a Groups Pro officer role with a read-only board. You can open Command and reports. You cannot assign Driver on Duty, seat the waitlist, or edit the hub.

Command Digest Packet No auto-email

Read-only Command

Club → Groups → Command. You see hubs, seats, needs, I’m here counts, still-out, Driver on Duty hours, and whether still-out closed. Glance tiles still open the hub. You do not get Fill leftover seats or assign duty.

Still-out is members whose check-out plan is Need a ride home. I’m here attendance is not still-out. Officers (not advisors) tap They’re in or No-show.

Advisor digest

An officer sends the digest in-app after night close (or on demand). You get counts, not a named attendance sheet:

  • Checked in (I’m here / hub check-ins in the period)
  • Seats offered, ride-needs filled
  • No-shows, cancelled listings
  • Driver-on-duty hours
  • Incidents logged (SOS counts only)
  • Per-hub check-in count and status

Chapter report is the same idea over a longer window: Hub check-ins as a count for officers and advisors.

Campus packet is an officer export

President or event chair exports the campus social packet: named attendees with check-in time, waitlist, seats, sober monitors, windows. FARU does not file it with the school. You may receive a copy if the chapter already owes you paper — that send is an officer choice.

FARU does not email the university

What you get

  • In-app digest when an officer sends it
  • Read-only Command and chapter report
  • Transition pack at gavel change (term history + snapshots)

What FARU will not do

  • Auto-email you or Student Affairs when a night closes
  • Dispatch members into cars
  • Treat Driver on Duty as Profile Designated Drivers
