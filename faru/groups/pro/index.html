The board officers actually use

Club → Groups → Command. Glance tiles open the hub or the matching SOS ride — they are not unlabeled blobs. Members never see this screen.

Officer roles

President

Billing, officer slate, transfer the gavel, notes, council, packet, and Command. Closes the night.

Event chair

Hubs, templates, joint nights, pin, waitlist, arrive/leave waves, rain plan, 24h / 2h reminders.

Ride chair

Seats vs needs, leftover-seat fill, area pings, Driver on Duty target, duty-night signups.

Advisor

Read-only Command, weekly digest, season snapshots. Cannot assign duty or close the night.

Live night

Still-out is a named list

Still-out is not “everyone who opened the hub.” It is members who checked in as Need a ride home and do not yet have a completed hub ride or an They’re in mark. Walk / drive / offer seats are roster-only.

  • Message that person in-app
  • They’re in — they got a seat
  • No-show — drop them from the live count
  • Glance still-out opens this hub, not a dead tile

Coverage

Driver on Duty — a night slot, not Profile DD

Officers set how many Drivers on Duty the hub needs. Members sign a duty night. Coverage short stays visible until the target is met. Offering seats in check-out plans posts a ride — it does not write someone into this slot.

  • Duty week + signups on the club calendar
  • Optional attach of a Profile sober-ride network
  • Profile Designated Drivers stay on Safety — ID, schedule, request flow

Leave window

Waves, waitlist, leftover seats

Pin the hub. Split leave into wave 1 and wave 2. Waitlist people who missed a car. One tap fills leftover seats and pings the people still on the list. 24h and 2h reminders go out before arrive; a leave-window ping fires when it is time to go.

Weather / venue

Rain plan, then night close

Move pickup to the covered loop, keep the leave waves, ping tagged members. When the night is over: expire leftover needs, snapshot the roster, draft the advisor digest. Night close never silently emails the school.

Advisor and campus — tap, never silent

Groups Pro can produce the paper a chapter already owes an advisor or Student Affairs. FARU does not file it and does not auto-send it.

Advisor digest

In-app, read-only: hubs, seats, needs, driver-on-duty hours, whether still-out closed. The advisor does not get an inbox dump from FARU. Officers send the digest when they mean to.

Campus packet

Export roster, sober monitors, and windows. Not filed with the school. If a campus license stores a Student Affairs inbox, an officer can tap Send. Nothing goes out on a timer.

Multi-club

Council Command

A council or umbrella org rolls member-club nights onto one board: which hubs are live, which club is coverage-short, how many are still out. Each club still owns its seats and duty. Glance opens that hub.

Handoff

Officer transition

Term history, season snapshots, transfer the gavel. New officers inherit Command — not a lost folder in last year’s DMs. Advisor stays read-only across the handoff.

Groups Pro FAQ

Who can open Command?

Host, president, event chair, and ride chair. Advisor is read-only. Members get the tonight card only.

What counts as still-out?

Checked in with check-out plan “need a ride,” minus a completed hub ride or They’re in. I’m here, walk, drive alone, and offer seats are not still-out.

Can offering seats make someone Driver on Duty?

No. That check-out plan opens I have seats on the hub. Duty is an officer assignment or a signed duty night.

Does FARU email Student Affairs when we close?

No. Close drafts the advisor digest. Packet is export-only. Send is an explicit officer tap only if a campus license stored the inbox.

Do we still need FARU Groups?

Pro includes Groups: hubs, QR, series, club-only listings. Start from Club → Groups. See the full Groups use-case page.
