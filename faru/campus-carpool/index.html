Campus carpool for ride home

Campus carpool is simple: students at the same school post seats or Ride Requests for trips they’re already taking — especially hometown weekends, Thanksgiving, and break. You chat first, meet on campus, and share the road (and gas) instead of booking a stranger Uber for 200 miles.

Why students look for it

  • Uber and Lyft get expensive on longer hometown trips.
  • Facebook and Discord rideshare groups are messy and hard to trust.
  • Parents want a safer story than “random driver from a generic app.”

How FARU fits

FARU is a campus message board for verified classmates — not a taxi company. Drivers fill empty seats; riders find a ride home. Core browse, chat, and safety tools stay free; FARU+ adds corridor alerts and Class Rides.
