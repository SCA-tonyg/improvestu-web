Program terms

  • Represent FARU professionally on campus (social, orgs, word of mouth).
  • Your existing FARU referral code is your ambassador code — we do not create a second code.
  • Referral milestones count only after referred students successfully verify during the challenge window.
  • Base pay ($50 / $50 / $50) is for completing Days 1–30, 31–60, and 61–90 responsibilities as defined by FARU.
  • Milestone bonuses (+$25 / +$50 / +$100 at 25 / 50 / 100) and the #1 bonus (+$250) follow challenge rules, including first-N caps.
  • FARU may pause or end the challenge, adjust ranks for fraud, or deactivate ambassadors who misuse the program.
  • Acceptance is not employment; payments are program rewards subject to eligibility and FARU verification.
