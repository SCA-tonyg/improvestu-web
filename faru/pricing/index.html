FARU pricing

Student plans and club plans

FARU is a free campus carpool board for verified students, with optional FARU+ membership — not a per-mile fee on every seat. FARU Groups and Groups Pro bill the club for hubs and officer tools. Members still ride for free.

For students

Free vs FARU+

Included

Free

Verified student account — no credit card required to start free

  • Browse open rides at your university
  • Post rides (up to 5 open at a time)
  • Request seats and post Ride Requests
  • Browse Events lists and post an event ride
  • Join a club — post seats and join a sober-ride network for free
  • In-app chat before you ride
  • SOS, emergency contacts, check-ins, report, and block

Most popular

Membership

FARU+

30-day free trial, then $4.99/mo or $49.99/yr Save 17% with annual

Less than one Uber ride home. FARU+ pays for itself on your first break trip.

Break-trip math vs per-ride apps

A 200-mile ride home on RideAlong: ~$35–50 per ride + 7.25% fee. On FARU+: $4.99/month, unlimited rides, no per-seat cut.

  • Browse universities within about 15 miles
  • Unlimited open ride posts
  • Quick Rides, Class Rides, After class, For you
  • Hometown and corridor match alerts
  • Events Near campus / For you ranking
  • Ad-free experience

Start from Membership in the app. Cancel in Apple or Google subscriptions before the trial ends to avoid charges.

For clubs

FARU Groups vs Groups Pro

Billed to the club on the Play / App Store. Separate from FARU+ so a student can hold both. Orgs pay for hubs and officer tools; members still post seats, request rides, and join a sober-ride network for free.

Club plan

FARU Groups

$9.99/mo · billed to the club

  • Event ride hubs with arrive and leave windows
  • QR page so members can offer seats or need a ride
  • Club ride series (club-owned weekly posts)
  • Members still ride and join a sober-ride network for free

Club plan

Groups Pro

$24.99/mo · billed to the club

Everything in Groups, plus officer tools.

  • Officer roles: president, event chair, ride chair, advisor
  • Command board, still-out, and campus packet
  • 24h / 2h reminders, leave-window pings, unfilled-seat alerts
  • Templates, joint hubs, pin, waitlist, arrive/leave waves
  • Driver week, area pings, Driver on Duty, I’m here / check-out plans
  • Club-private notes and advisor (read-only) view
  • Sober-ride network attach and duty nights
  • Council seat roll-up for multi-club orgs

Start from Club → Groups in the app. Host or president can redeem a club code if one was issued. Full use cases and screens · Groups Pro / Command
