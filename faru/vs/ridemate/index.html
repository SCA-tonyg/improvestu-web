FARU vs Ridemate

Ridemate is a strong student intercity carpool option. FARU is built as a campus message board for verified classmates — ride home for break and weekends, Class Rides during the school day, and chat-first safety.

Topic FARU Ridemate
Focus Campus board + hometown / break ride home + Class Rides Student intercity carpool (“one ride, two wins”)
Empty seats / split gas Drivers post open seats; split gas in chat; no Stripe gig payouts Emphasizes cost and empty seats for longer trips
Who you ride with Verified students at your school (FARU+ nearby campuses) Students matched for shared routes
Pricing shape Free core; FARU+ $4.99/mo or $49.99/yr (save 17% annual) Check Ridemate for current offer
Safety tools Chat first, meet on campus, SOS/guardians/check-ins on free “Know who you ride with” framing
