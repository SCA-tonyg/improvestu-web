FARU vs Ridemate

Campus life > just another carpool booking

Ridemate is a verified-student carpool with in-app Stripe payments and a big push on intercity “one ride, two wins.” FARU is the modern student-union pinboard: classmates at your school, chat first, meet on campus, ride home for break — plus Class Rides when you need a seat across campus, not only across the state.

Bottom line

Ridemate books and bills the seat through the app. FARU keeps money between classmates and puts the free board, safety stack, Designated Drivers, and schedule-aware Class Rides where students actually live — on campus.

Where FARU pulls ahead

  • You’re not stuck in a payment middleman. Ridemate routes cost-share through Stripe (“confirm & pay in-app”). FARU doesn’t process ride payments or take a seat cut — agree on gas in chat like real classmates.
  • Built for campus rhythm, not only intercity. Class Rides, Quick Rides, campus event rides, and hometown corridor alerts sit next to break trips home. Ridemate’s story centers on shared long routes; FARU covers the board you check between classes too.
  • Free tier with teeth. Verified students browse, post limited rides, request seats, post Ride Requests, chat, and use SOS / emergency contacts / check-ins free. You can try the board before anyone asks for a card.
  • Drivers aren’t onboarding into payouts. Fill empty seats, split gas, no Stripe payout flow, no gig paperwork. Cost recovery — not a side hustle portal.
  • Safety that parents can understand. Chat first, meet on campus, report/block, live location with emergency contacts, Designated Driver opt-in — tools that match “show my parents this isn’t random Uber.”
  • Predictable membership vs booking friction. FARU+ is $4.99/mo or $49.99/yr (save 17% annual) after a 30-day trial — less than one Uber ride home. Unlock wider browse and corridor alerts without paying the app on every seat.

When Ridemate might still make sense

If you want the app to lock the seat and charge the card for every intercity hop, Ridemate’s book-and-pay flow is aimed there. If you want the pinboard for your campus — ride home for break, empty seats, Class Rides, and safety without a payment middleman — FARU is the superior fit.
