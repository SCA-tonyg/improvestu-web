FARU vs RideAlong

Both help students avoid strangers and Uber prices on campus trips. FARU is a classmate message board with a free tier and optional FARU+ membership. RideAlong is known for a per-ride pricing model (base + per-mile style fees).

Topic FARU RideAlong
Model Campus message board; free core + FARU+ membership Student ride network with per-ride pricing
Ride home / hometown Hometown and break trips are a primary use case Strong “ride home with students” positioning
Cost for riders Free to start; FARU+ $4.99/mo or $49.99/yr — less than one Uber ride home Pay per trip (base + distance-style fees)
Payments between riders No — students arrange gas share in chat; FARU takes no seat cut Platform pricing / fees on rides
Safety framing Verified classmates, chat first, meet on campus, SOS/guardians on free Students-not-strangers positioning
