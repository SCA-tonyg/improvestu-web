FARU vs Uber

Classmates for ride home. Not a metered stranger.

Uber is built to dispatch a driver to you and bill by the mile. FARU (Find A Ride University) is a campus carpool board for verified students — chat first, meet on campus, split gas with someone already going your way for break or the weekend.

Bottom line

For a 200-mile ride home, Uber’s meter climbs fast. FARU keeps the free board, safety tools, and gas share between classmates — with optional FARU+ at $4.99/mo after a 30-day trial, often less than one Uber ride home.

Where FARU pulls ahead

  • Price for long trips. Uber bills distance and time. FARU riders split gas with classmates; FARU takes $0 of the seat.
  • Who’s in the car. FARU is verified students at your university. Uber pairs you with a professional driver you don’t know.
  • How the trip starts. FARU: chat in-app, meet on campus, leave together. Uber: request → driver arrives at your pin.
  • Safety without a taxi model. SOS, emergency contacts, check-ins, report, and block ship on FARU’s free tier.
  • Campus life, not only “get me somewhere now.” FARU+ adds Class Rides, Quick Rides, and hometown corridor alerts — built for the school week and break.

Quick answers

Is FARU cheaper than Uber for ride home?

For long hometown and break trips, yes. You share gas with classmates instead of paying a full metered fare. FARU+ after trial is still usually less than one Uber ride home.

Is FARU the same as Uber?

No. FARU does not dispatch drivers or process ride payments. It’s a board for verified classmates at your school.

When should I still use Uber?

Short on-demand trips with no matching classmate — airports with no board post, last-minute runs, or when you need a professional driver immediately.
